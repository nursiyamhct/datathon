IMPORTANT FOR ALL TEAMS

Responsible AI: Use It. Check It. Own It.

AI tools are welcome, but your team must understand, verify and be able to defend the work. Use these rules when preparing and competing.

🟢 Allowed

Ask AI to explain forecasting concepts, debug Python or RapidMiner errors, or improve slide wording.

🟡 Allowed — verify

Use AI to write or change code and interpret results, but test everything and be able to explain it.

🔴 Not allowed

Fabricate results, share personal data or another team's work, or submit work you cannot explain.

Required: AI disclosure in three steps

  1. Log it: record the AI tool and what it helped with.
  2. Check it: test AI-assisted code and findings yourself.
  3. Disclose it: add an AI appendix slide to your final presentation, describing the tool, its contribution, and how you checked it.

The appendix is an extra slide, outside the three-slide presentation limit. No full prompt log is required. Never upload personal data, another team's work, or your team's upload code to AI tools.

Competition Guidelines

Helpful reminders for all participating teams.

💻

Bring your laptop

Every participant should bring a laptop. Wi-Fi and power connections will be provided at the venue. Bring your charger.

📊

Choose your tools

You may use Python, RapidMiner, Tableau, Power BI or other suitable tools for data preparation, analysis, modeling and visualization.

✦

Responsible AI use

LLMs and generative AI tools are permitted, provided their use is transparent, documented and critically reviewed.

Using LLMs responsibly

  • Disclose and cite any LLM or AI tools used in your work.
  • Include representative prompts demonstrating how AI supported your analysis or deliverables.
  • Review and verify AI-generated code, explanations, figures and recommendations before submission.
  • Take responsibility for the accuracy, originality, privacy and ethical use of all submitted work.
